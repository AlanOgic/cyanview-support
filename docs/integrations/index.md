Connect and control a wide range of camera models across dozens of manufacturers. Use the sections below to find setup instructions for your specific camera, lens, accessory, or broadcast system.

:::tip Not sure where to start?
Check the [generic guides](#generic-guides) first — they cover the underlying connection principles and apply to many devices, even those not listed by brand.
:::

## Generic guides

These guides cover connection types and protocols rather than specific models. Start here if your device is not listed, or if you want to understand how Cyanview handles each signal type.

- [Serial cameras](/docs/integrations/generic/serial-camera) — Sony 8-pin, LANC, VISCA RS-232/422
- [IP cameras](/docs/integrations/generic/ip-camera) — Any camera with an Ethernet connection
- [PTZ cameras](/docs/integrations/generic/ptz) — Pan-tilt-zoom cameras over IP or serial
- [Lenses](/docs/integrations/generic/lens) — Motorized lens control (B4, cine servo)
- [Routers and switchers](/docs/integrations/generic/routers) — Tally and auxiliary routing
- [Color correctors](/docs/integrations/generic/color-corrector) — Standalone or paired with cameras
- [3D LUT processors](/docs/integrations/generic/3d-lut-processor) — LUT-based color management
